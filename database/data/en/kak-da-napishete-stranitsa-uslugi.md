How to Write a Services Page That Brings Enquiries
how-to-write-a-services-page
A list of general words does not sell. Here is how to describe your services so the customer understands what they get and calls you.
---
The services page is where people decide whether you are right for them. Too often it holds a list such as "Professional service. Quality. Reliability." Everybody writes the same thing, so it says nothing.

## Start with the customer's problem

Nobody searches for "waterproofing". They want the ceiling to stop leaking. Start with that. "Is your roof leaking? We come to inspect it and tell you exactly what needs to be done."

Think about the sentence your customers use when they phone you, because that is the best opening for the text. "My car shakes when I brake" becomes "Vibrations when braking? We check the brakes and suspension within a day." "I want my teeth whitened before a wedding" becomes "Teeth whitening in one or two visits, with a clear price in advance." "I need an accountant, I am opening a company" becomes "Opening a company? We help with registration and keep your books from the first month."

## Answer four questions for every service

First, what is it? Say it in one sentence without professional jargon. Second, who is it for? For example, apartments and houses in Plovdiv and up to 30 kilometres from the city. Third, what does it include? A short mention of the inspection, the materials and the removal of waste. Fourth, what does it cost, or what does the price depend on? Even "from 40 euro per square metre, exact price after inspection" is better than nothing.

Add a fifth, which is how it works. People worry about the unknown, and a short description of the steps removes the worry. You call or send a photo, we come to inspect within two working days, you receive a written quote, and we start on the agreed day and clean up after ourselves.

## Show, do not promise

Instead of "we work with quality" put a before and after photo. Instead of "we react fast" write something concrete such as "We reply within one working day." One concrete sentence says more than three nice words.

Other ways to show are a short case, such as a house roof in Brestnik of 120 square metres where the waterproofing was replaced in four days. You can put a review from a customer who used exactly this service next to the service itself. You can also mention certificates or training if they matter to the customer, for example for medical or technical services.

## Answer the objections

Under each main service add three or four questions customers ask before they decide. How long does it take? Do I have to be at home? Is there a guarantee? Can I pay in instalments? These questions and answers also help Google and AI assistants understand exactly what you offer.

## One page for everything or one page per service

If you have two or three simple services, one page with clear sections is enough. If you have several main services that people search for separately, such as straightening wheels and changing tyres, it is better to give each its own page. Then every page can have its own title for Google and appear for its own search. You can also write in more detail without overloading the general page, and you can send people straight to the page they need.

## One clear action under every service

Under each service there should be a button or a phone number, such as "Ask for a price" or "Book an inspection". Do not make the person search for where to write to you.

## Write the way you speak

Read the text out loud. If it sounds like a contract, rewrite it. Your customers are people, not a committee. A few words you can almost always delete are professional, quality, innovative, individual approach and wide range. If the sentence makes sense without them, remove them.

## An example

Before, you might write "We offer a wide range of cosmetic services at a high level."

After, you might write "Facial cleansing, 60 minutes, 45 euro. Suitable for clogged pores and after the summer. You book by phone or through the form."

A whole service could read like this. "Roof repair in Plovdiv. Is your roof leaking, or did tiles fall after the storm? We come to inspect it and tell you exactly what needs doing and what it will cost. The service includes the inspection, replacement of tiles, gutters and waterproofing, and removal of waste. The price starts from 25 euro per square metre, with the exact price after the inspection. Most repairs take two to five days. Request an inspection."

## Common questions

**How long should the text for one service be?** As long as it takes to answer the customer's questions. A few sentences are enough for a simple service. For a complex and expensive one, a longer page with examples and questions usually sells better.

**Should I write prices if every case is different?** Yes, at least approximately or as "from" a figure. People who see no price at all often assume it is expensive and do not ask.
