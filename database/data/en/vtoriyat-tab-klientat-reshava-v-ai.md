The Second Tab and How Customers Check You in AI Before They Buy
the-second-tab-customers-check-you-in-ai
A customer clicks an ad, opens a new tab and asks AI whether to trust you. If AI does not mention you, you do not exist for them. What to do today.
---
Imagine a person looking at a Facebook ad for a remote control duplicator at 15.99 euro instead of 30.99 euro. They click. Then they open a new tab and ask AI whether this is a good shop and whether there is a better one. The decision is made there and not on your website.

Adrian Nikolov of Haide Digital, who has spent 17 years in search, calls it the second tab. At Digital Community Day 2026 he showed what it means for businesses that pay for ads and then wonder why the orders do not come.

## Three possible answers

When a customer asks AI about you, there are three outcomes. In the first, AI confirms you and the customer buys. In the second, AI recommends a competitor. Nikolov puts it bluntly, saying that in that case you paid for their sale. In the third, AI says nothing. In his words, silence equals non existence.

The third outcome is the sneakiest, because you see it in no report. There is no click and no bounce, just a customer who never reached you.

## How AI decides whom to trust

AI does not look only at your website. It splits the question into several searches, reads the pages it can reach and assembles what the sources agree on. If your site shows one price, a marketplace another and a comparison site a third, the signal breaks.

Nikolov takes the story back to PageRank in 1998, when every link was a vote. He adds that back then the vote was a link, and today it is a review, an article, a forum post or a video.

## What the numbers show

All the numbers below come from the speaker's slides, with the source he gives. Mentions across the web follow visibility in AI about three times more strongly than links do, according to Ahrefs in a study of 75,000 brands. Of the brands, 89 percent are missing when AI recommends in their category, according to Victorious in a study of 175 brands. About 56 percent of visits after an AI recommendation come from a search for the brand, according to Similarweb and SparkToro. Of Bulgarians, 22.5 percent use AI, against 32.7 percent on average in the EU, according to Eurostat in December 2025. ChatGPT has shown ads in Bulgaria since 24 August 2026. According to OpenAI the advertising is kept separate from the answer, and an advertiser cannot pay to change it.

Paid advertising is meanwhile getting more expensive. The average cost of an ad on Meta was 14 percent higher in the third quarter of 2026 than in the same quarter of 2025, according to Meta's Q3 2026 results. Google Performance Max had a 46 percent lower ROAS in the EU and the EEA in June 2026 than in June 2025, according to Channable, which looked at more than 10,000 advertisers.

Nikolov adds one clarification. Google Search Central says that for AI Overviews and AI Mode there are no additional requirements or special optimisations. There is no trick, only good basic work done well.

## Three things to do

**First, answer every customer question in text on your site.** Nikolov cites Marcus Sheridan of River Pools and his idea of "They ask, you answer". People ask about price, problems, comparisons, reviews and which option is best. Write a price, or at least "from" a figure. Write an honest comparison. Write who you are not suitable for, because that makes both AI and people trust you more.

**Second, get others to talk about you.** That means Google reviews, mentions in the media, video, forums, shops and comparison sites. The most important thing is that your details are the same everywhere, including name, address, prices and opening hours.

**Third, check the second tab.** The speaker sums it up as checking the second tab before you pay for the first. Before you pay for advertising, see what AI says.

## A test you can do today

It takes 15 minutes and is free. Open ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity. Ask as a customer would, for example who offers your service in your city, how much your service costs, and whether there are good reviews for your business name. Write down what they answer, including whether they mention you, with what details, and whom they recommend instead.

That is your starting point. Repeat it after two or three months. Do not expect a jump within a week, because AI collects signals from many places and they build up slowly.

If you want to start from the basics, [check your website for free](/en/website-check). You will see whether there is a clear title, a description, structured data and a phone number in a visible place. That is the minimum both people and machines read.

The speaker's best comparison is a short one. Ads are rent, and authority is a mortgage. With rent you stop paying and it is gone. With authority you build slowly, but it stays yours.
