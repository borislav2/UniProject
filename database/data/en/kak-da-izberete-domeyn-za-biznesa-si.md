How to Choose a Domain Name for Your Business
how-to-choose-a-domain-name
Should it be .bg or .com, with a hyphen or without, in Latin letters? Short rules for choosing a domain name you will not regret in a year.
---
Choosing a domain name is one of the first decisions when you build a website, and one of the hardest to change later. The domain is the address of your website, for example creatiumlab.com. In time it ends up on business cards, in Google, in your profiles and in your customers' heads, so it is worth giving it some thought at the start. This article gives short rules for choosing a domain, the difference between .bg and .com, and a few things people often miss.

## What a domain is and why the choice matters

The domain is the name people type into the browser to reach your site. Your company email is tied to it too, for example info@yourcompany.bg. When you change it, you have to redirect the old address, update your profiles and tell your customers. A good choice now saves a lot of work in a year.

A domain does not lift you in Google by itself. Search engines look at your content, your speed and your profile, not at how nice the address sounds. But the domain does affect trust, and whether people remember it and type it correctly.

## Keep it short and easy to say over the phone

Try dictating it to someone. If you have to explain that it has two letters here, a hyphen there and then dot bg, look for another one. A good domain gets typed correctly the first time.

Here is the test we use. Say the domain out loud to a person who does not know it and ask them to write it down. If they write it wrongly, your customers will too. When you hesitate between two names, the one that is easier to say always wins.

## Choosing between .bg and .com

If you work only in Bulgaria, a .bg domain is a good choice because it shows you are a local business, and customers in the country recognise it at once. A .com is right if you have customers abroad or if the .bg is already taken. What matters is to pick one and use it everywhere.

If the budget allows, register both and redirect one to the other. That way nobody who gets the ending wrong lands on someone else's site. Other endings such as .eu, .net, .shop or .studio work technically, but people remember them less easily and type .com or .bg out of habit. If you choose one of those, take the .bg version too when it is free.

## Latin letters, and no hyphens when possible

Cyrillic domains exist, but they are hard to type and share, and in some apps the links break. Pick a transliteration that people would write themselves. A domain such as "zdravpokriv" is better than "zdrav-pokriv-bg-ltd".

Be careful with letters that can be transliterated in more than one way. In Bulgarian these include the letters for zh or j, ts or c, the vowel that becomes a or u, and sht. If your name contains them, check which variant feels more natural, and if possible register the other one as well and let it redirect to the main one.

## The name of the business or the name of the service

The business name is the better long term choice because it is unique and yours. A domain like "roofs-plovdiv" does not lift you in Google the way many people think. What counts is the content of the site and your Google profile. We have a separate article about the second one, [how to set up your Google Business Profile](/en/blog/google-business-profile-setup).

There is also a practical problem. If in a year you start working in another city or add a service, a domain with the old city or service no longer describes you.

## In whose name the domain is registered

This is the most common mistake we see. The domain must be registered to your company and with your email, not to the person who built your website. Otherwise, if you part ways, you may find that the address is not yours.

To check, sign in to your account at the registrar and see who is listed as the owner. If you have no account and do not know where the domain is registered, sort that out now and not when it expires.

## Do not forget the renewal

A domain is paid for every year. Turn on automatic renewal and make sure the reminder email goes to an address someone actually reads. An expired domain means a stopped website and stopped email.

If a domain expires and you do not renew it in time, after a grace period it is released and anyone can buy it. Some people watch for exactly such domains and then sell them back to their owners at a much higher price.

## The domain and your company email

The domain is not only for the website. With it you get email in your company name, such as info@yourcompany.bg instead of yourcompany@gmail.com. It looks more professional and your messages land in spam less often. If you already use a free mailbox, you do not have to close it at once. You can forward messages from the new address to the old one until you get used to it.

## What to do if you already have a domain

If your domain is not perfect, do not rush to change it. First check whose name it is registered in and when it expires. Then honestly judge whether it is easy to write and whether people remember it. A change is worth it only when the name confuses customers or when the domain is not yours. In that case register the new one, redirect the old one to it and update your profiles.

## A short checklist before you buy

Make sure it is spelled correctly after one hearing, and that it has no hyphens or digits unless they are part of your name. It should not be too close to a competitor or a famous brand. It must be registered to your company with your email, with automatic renewal on. If possible, have both the .bg and the .com.

## Common questions

**Can I change the domain later?** Yes, but it means work. You need redirects from the old address, changes in Google Search Console and in your Google profile, and updates on business cards and everywhere the address is published. That is why it is better to choose carefully at the start.

**How much does a domain cost?** The price depends on the registrar and the ending. When you compare, look not only at the first year but also at the renewal price. If someone offers a free domain with hosting, check in whose name it is registered and what the renewal will cost.

**Are the domain and the hosting the same thing?** No. The domain is the address and the hosting is the server where the site lives. They can be with different companies. You can read more about hosting in our [maintenance and hosting](/en/services/maintenance-and-hosting) service.

**Should I pick .bg if I sell only in Bulgaria?** In most cases yes. A .bg domain is recognised by Bulgarian customers. If you plan to sell abroad as well, think about a .com and connect the two.
