Near Me Searches in Google and How to Appear on the Map
near-me-searches-how-to-appear-on-google-maps
Local search follows its own rules. Here is what really decides whether Google shows you on the map for searches like near me.
---
Locksmith near me, pizza in Lozenets, vet in Varna open now. When people search like this, Google shows a map with a few local businesses. The order depends mainly on three things, which are proximity, relevance and prominence.

These map results often sit above the ordinary results and receive a large share of the phone calls. For a local business, being there matters more than almost anything else in Google.

## 1. Proximity

This is how far the business is from the person searching, or from the place they typed. You cannot change it, but you can tell Google exactly where you are and which areas you serve.

That is why the same business can be first for a person in Lozenets and fifth for a person in Lyulin. It is normal and does not mean anything is broken. When you check where you appear, keep in mind where you are searching from.

## 2. Relevance

This is how well your profile matches the search, and here you have the most control. Choose the **exact main category** in Google Business Profile. Describe your services in the words your customers use. On your website, create a separate page or section for each main service and for each city if you work in several. Do not copy the same text with only the name of the city changed.

The main category is one of the strongest signals. If you are a car service but people mostly search for tyres, consider whether a secondary category such as tyre shop fits. Choose the categories that truly describe what you do and not every one that sounds close.

Use your customers' words and not your own. You say dental implantology and the customers search for implants. You say plumbing services and they search for plumber. Use both versions in the description, in the services section of the profile and on the website.

## 3. Prominence

This is how well known and reliable the business looks. Reviews help, meaning their number, the rating and how recent the last one is. Mentions on local sites, directories and in the media help too. The same details everywhere help, with name, address and phone written in the same way. And links to your site from other sites help, especially local ones such as municipal sites, trade organisations, partners and local media.

Directories where it is worth having the same details include the Bulgarian business directories, trade sites for doctors, lawyers or tourism, Apple Maps, Bing Maps and your Facebook page. You do not need to be everywhere. What matters is that where you are listed, the details are correct and identical.

## A quick checklist

Check that your Google Business Profile is verified and fully filled in, and that the opening hours are correct including holidays. Your website should mention the city and the district where you work, and the address and phone on the site should match those in Google. You should get new reviews at least once a month and answer them. Each main service should have its own page, and your details in directories should match those in Google.

## What does not help

Stuffing the text with "near me" or with the names of all the districts does not help. Google understands what "near me" means and takes the person's location by itself.

Keywords in the profile name do not help either. A name such as "Dentist Sofia Center Cheap" instead of the real name breaks the rules and can get the profile suspended. A fake address or a virtual office, used just to look closer to the centre, also fails, because Google checks and such profiles are often suspended. And many profiles for one business do not help. One business at one address has one profile.

## How to check where you appear

If you search from your own phone, Google shows results based on your location and history. For a more accurate picture, open a browser in incognito mode and search for the service with the name of the city, for example "car service Burgas". See whether you are on the map and at which place. Then repeat it for your main services.

In your Google Business Profile, the performance section also shows which searches Google found you for and how many people asked for directions or called you.

## How long it takes

Changes to the profile show up quickly, sometimes within days. Improving your position usually takes weeks to months, especially if you need to collect more reviews and mentions. Do not believe people who promise first place on the map in seven days.

## Common questions

**Do I have to pay Google to be on the map?** No, the map is free. There are also paid ads that appear at the top marked as sponsored, but they are separate.

**I work from home. Will Google show me?** Yes, as a service area business. You hide the address and list the districts where you work.

**Why is a competitor with worse reviews above me?** Most often because they are closer to where the search is made, or because their category and description match the search better. Reviews matter, but they are not the only factor.
