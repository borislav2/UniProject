Google Business Profile Setup for Local Businesses
google-business-profile-setup
When someone searches for a dentist in Varna, Google shows a map of businesses first. Here is how to appear there with full and correct details.
---
When someone searches for "car service Burgas" or "hairdresser near me", Google shows a map with a few businesses before the ordinary results. Those businesses come from **Google Business Profile**. The profile is free, and for many local businesses it brings more phone calls than the website itself.

Here is how to set it up properly the first time, step by step.

## Step one is to create or claim the profile

Sign in at business.google.com with your company email and search for your business. Sometimes a profile already exists, created by Google or by a customer. In that case request ownership of it. Google will confirm it with a code by post, by phone or with a short video.

For the video, Google wants to see that the business is real. Film the sign or the entrance from outside, then the inside, and something that shows you work there, such as tools, a till or a workspace. It does not have to look good, it has to be clear.

Use a company email, or one that both owners can access. Do not hand the profile to someone else's email, even to the person who helps with your marketing. If needed, add them as a manager, and keep ownership yourself.

## Step two is to fill in everything, not only the name

Use the exact name of the business as it appears on the sign. Do not add words like "Best Car Service Burgas". That breaks the rules and can get the profile suspended.

Choose the most precise main category, for example "Dental clinic" rather than just "Health". You can add secondary categories too. If you travel to customers, as roofers and plumbers do, list the towns you serve instead of showing your home address. Include opening hours, and holiday hours as well, because nothing annoys people more than a closed door when Google says open.

Give the same phone number and website as on your site, written the same way. Write a description of two or three sentences about what you do and for whom, without a list of keywords. Use the services section of the profile to add your main services with a short description and a price where possible. Add attributes such as wheelchair access, parking, card payment and Wi-Fi, because people filter by them.

Here is an example of a good description. "A family car service in the Meden Rudnik district of Burgas. We repair engines, suspension and brakes on passenger cars and change tyres and oil. We work from Monday to Saturday and give a written quote before any repair." It is short and concrete, and it uses the words people search for.

## Step three is photos

Upload a photo of the entrance from outside so that people can find you, and photos of the interior, the team and your work. Phone photos are perfectly fine as long as they are bright and clear. A logo as a square and a horizontal cover photo complete the set.

Add a few new photos every month. Profiles with regular new photos look alive.

## Step four is reviews

Ask happy customers for a review right after the service, while they are still happy. Send them the direct link to the review form. Answer every review, including the bad ones, calmly and to the point.

A good reply is short, personal and mentions something specific from the review. For a bad review, stay calm, acknowledge the problem and offer to solve it by phone.

## Step five is to keep the profile alive

Update the opening hours when they change, add new photos and answer questions in the profile. An active profile looks more reliable to people and to Google. You can also publish short posts in the profile about a new service, a change of hours or a seasonal offer. They show up directly in Google when someone finds you.

## Common mistakes

The most frequent one is two profiles for one business. It happens when someone creates a new profile instead of claiming the old one, and Google may suspend both. If you have a duplicate, ask support to merge them.

A different address or phone number in the profile and on the website confuses both people and Google. Keywords in the business name look like a clever trick, but they are the most common reason a profile gets suspended. Finally, a profile created by a former employee or by an agency you no longer work with is a risk, so check who has access under the people and access settings and remove anyone who should not be there.

## Common questions

**Can I have a profile if I have no office?** Yes. If you serve customers at their address, choose a service area business and hide the address. List the towns and districts where you work.

**How long until I appear on the map?** After confirmation the profile usually shows within days. How high it ranks depends on proximity, how well it is filled in and your reviews.

**Does it cost anything?** The profile is free. You pay only if you decide to run Google ads.
