Blog for a Small Business and What to Write About
blog-for-a-small-business-what-to-write-about
Think you have nothing to say in a blog? The best topics are ones you already hear from your customers every day. Here is how to spot and use them.
---
Almost every owner you ask whether they have a blog answers in the same way. They say they have nothing to write, that their work is boring to read about, or that the competition has already written everything. That is rarely true, but the feeling is understandable, because it is hard to see how much you know when you do it every day.

The truth is that you know many things your customers do not. Those are exactly the things they search for in Google before they call you.

## Start from your customers' questions

The best source of topics is your phone. Think about what you are asked most often. A dentist hears how much a filling will hurt and how long to wait before eating. An accountant hears which documents to prepare for the annual return. The owner of a guest house in Bansko hears how to reach the house in winter and whether there is parking.

Every such question is a ready made topic. Answer it the way you would to a customer standing in front of you. You do not need pretty phrases, just a clear and honest answer.

## Write about the mistakes people make

The second rich topic is the things customers get wrong. A car service can explain which dashboard signals must not be ignored and which are harmless. A hairdresser can explain what happens when a person colours their hair at home and why a repair is sometimes needed.

Texts like these are useful because they help a person avoid a problem, and that builds trust. The reader thinks that if you know the pitfalls, you will probably cope with their case too. But be careful not to sound preachy. The tone of a colleague sharing experience is far more pleasant than the tone of a teacher.

## Tell how you work on the inside

People like to know what happens behind the scenes. How a day goes at your place, how an order is prepared, how you choose materials or suppliers. You take these things for ordinary, but for the customer they are interesting and show that you know your craft.

If you plan to mention a specific case, change the details so the customer cannot be recognised, and always ask permission. Do not invent stories. Readers sense when something is made up, and you will also have to remember it later.

## Explain how to choose

Many people do not know what to compare when choosing a firm like yours. Help them. How to choose a good accountant, what to look at in a quote for a renovation, what questions to ask before agreeing. That shows that you are honest, and the person who contacts you will probably be the one who valued your honesty.

## How to write an article when you are not a writer

You do not need to write like a writer. Imagine you are explaining something to a customer on the phone, and write down what you would say. Then arrange it into a few paragraphs and add a title that contains the words a person would use to search for the topic. You can even dictate it into your phone and polish it afterwards.

Read the finished text and remove everything you would not say out loud. If a sentence sounds like a brochure, replace it with an ordinary one. People trust a text that sounds like a person more easily.

## How often and how long

You do not need to write every day. One good article every two weeks is more than enough for a small business, and even one a month is fine if you keep it up for a long time. What matters more is that you write real things that someone might search for.

Length is not the most important thing either. A text of eight hundred words that clearly answers one question is better than one twice as long that circles the topic.

A blog does not bring customers in a week. It takes time, and the results cannot be guaranteed, however well you write. So treat the blog as a long term investment of your time and not as a quick way to get customers. But over the years the articles you have built up become a real stock that works for you while you do something else. They also help when assistants and search engines try to work out what you do.
