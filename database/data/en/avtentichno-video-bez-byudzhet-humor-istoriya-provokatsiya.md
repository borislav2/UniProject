Authentic Video Without a Budget for Small Businesses
authentic-video-without-a-budget
Videos with over a million views and no ad spend. What humour, story, provocation and comments have in common, and how to start with a phone.
---
One million paid video views cost about 5,000 euro if you count 0.005 euro per view. That is a rough calculation from a slide, with no source given, but it shows the scale. Maria Gulisheva, founder of MOGIC DIGITAL, showed videos at Digital Community Day 2026 that reached a million views with zero euro of ad budget.

Her main thought was that the right idea beats a big budget.

## What the examples are

The numbers come from the speaker's slides, with no outside source. A gift shop reached 1.1 million views on a budget of zero euro. The seed brand Botanika reached 1 million views, also on zero euro. Videos about backpacks passed 260,000 views.

Zero euro means no paid advertising. The work on the idea, filming and editing still takes time, and time has a price. But you do not need a big budget for a small brand to be heard.

## The four levers

Gulisheva reduces it to four, which are humour, story, provocation and comments.

**Humour.** The BackToSchool campaign of UWEAR.bg takes on the parent's sore point, which is whether the backpack will last. An employee and a child test a backpack in a funny way. The question is real and the manner is light.

**Story.** Tell instead of show. Take the homemade ice cream at the mountain lodge Hizha Kuker. Instead of showing only the product, the video tells a story around it.

**Provocation.** The new brand Sany has a repeating format that starts with the words "They are robbing you, but you are a mother." The format repeats, so the viewer recognises it from the first words. There is also a paid partnership with influencers, so not everything is organic.

**Comments.** People respond to a video that provokes them, and the conversation continues under the clip. Reply to comments, ask viewers questions and leave something unsaid.

## Not only for products

Organic TikTok also works for less obvious things, the speaker shows, such as a gutter machine from VAS and hiring staff. If you sell a boring product or are looking for people, there is still a way. Show how it works, who makes it and what goes wrong.

## Why authenticity matters more than ever

One warning from the slides is that 59 percent of videos for a new TikTok account are what Kapwing calls AI slop, meaning low quality content made with AI. When the feed fills up with identical synthetic clips, a real person with a real story stands out even more.

## What to film, with ideas for different businesses

Here are questions customers often ask, each of which can become a video of 30 to 60 seconds. The ideas are ours, as examples. A car service could answer why a repair notice arrived when the car seems fine. A beauty salon could say how often a treatment should be done and when it is unnecessary. An online shop could test whether a product holds up by throwing, wetting and pulling it, which is a direct continuation of the backpack example. A building company could explain what it costs you if you do not ask about a permit.

In every case the question comes from the customer and the answer from the person who knows. That is enough for a start.

## How to start with a phone

First, take one question from a customer, something you are asked often, such as whether it lasts, how long delivery takes or why it costs more. Second, answer it in 30 to 60 seconds, with one person and one shot and no staged script, and show the product itself. Third, add one of the four levers, which is a joke, a small story, a bold claim or a question to the viewers. Fourth, reply to the comments, even with a new video. Fifth, repeat the format. Sany does not invent a new format each time, and a repeating frame is easier to make and easier to recognise. Sixth, measure. Look at views, saves, comments and, most important, whether people ask about the product. Views alone do not pay the bills.

## What not to expect

The speaker shows her best examples and not the average result. Most videos will get few views, and that is normal. So think of a series of clips and not of one perfect video. After each video ask whether enquiries, calls or orders came. If they did not, change the question you answer and not the camera.

We do not promise that every video will reach a million. But unlike advertising, every attempt costs you time and not money, and you learn what your audience likes.
