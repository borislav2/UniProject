Website Enquiry Form Mistakes That Lose You Customers
website-enquiry-form-mistakes
A visitor liked your service and decided to write, yet sometimes nothing arrives. Here are the six common mistakes behind that.
---
The enquiry form is the last step between interest and a customer. If something gets in the way there, you lose people who had already decided to write to you. Here are the mistakes we see most often and how to fix them.

## 1. Too many required fields

First name, last name, company, tax number, address, phone, email, budget. Every field is a reason to give up. For a first enquiry, **a name, a phone or an email and one box for a message** are enough. You will learn the rest in the conversation.

If you need more information to give a quote, make the extra fields optional or ask later by phone. The exception is a business where you cannot answer without the details, such as dates for a hotel. Then ask only for that. Do not demand an exact phone format either. People write the number the way they are used to, so the form should accept them all.

## 2. The form is broken and nobody knows

This is the most expensive mistake. Enquiries go to an email nobody reads, or to spam, or never arrive at all. Send yourself a test enquiry every month.

The most common causes are an address that belongs to a former employee or to the person who built the site, a site that sends mail from an address your mailbox treats as spam, a plugin or update that broke the sending, and a full mailbox. A good solution is to keep the enquiries inside the website as well, and not only send them by email. That way even if the email fails, the enquiry is not lost.

## 3. There is no confirmation

The person presses Send and nothing happens. Was it sent? Should they try again? Show a clear message such as "We received your enquiry and will call you within one working day." And keep that promise. Even better, send the person a short automatic email with a copy of the enquiry and a phone number in case they want you to call sooner.

## 4. It is hard to fill in on a phone

Small fields, a keyboard that covers the button, a phone field that opens letters instead of digits. Fill in your own form from your phone and you will see. On a phone the fields should be big enough for a finger. The phone field should open a numeric keyboard, and the email field a keyboard with the at sign. The browser should be able to fill in the name and phone automatically, and the Send button should be visible without closing the keyboard.

## 5. A CAPTCHA that annoys

"Select all the traffic lights" puts people off. There are invisible ways to protect against spam, such as a hidden field that bots fill in and people never see. If spam is a big problem, there are more modern invisible checks that work in the background and show a task only when the behaviour looks suspicious.

## 6. The form is hidden

If it is only on the Contacts page and the person decided to write while reading about a service, they will have to look for it. Put a button to the form everywhere a person might decide. Good places are the end of every service page, next to prices, after the reviews and in the top menu.

## 7. It is unclear what happens next

A button that says Send enquiry tells nothing about what comes after. People worry that you will flood them with calls or ask for money for a quote. Add one sentence next to the button, for example "We will call you within one working day to clarify the details."

## 8. Consent for personal data

The form must have a link to the privacy policy. If you also want to send marketing emails, you need a **separate** checkbox for it, not ticked in advance. Do not make consent for marketing mandatory for sending an enquiry.

## Bonus, which is to reply fast

A quick call after an enquiry makes a huge impression. The customer has often written to several companies and chooses the one that calls first. If you cannot answer at once, at least send a short message such as "We received your enquiry and will call you tomorrow before noon." The person knows they have not been forgotten.

## How to check your form in five minutes

Open the site on your phone and find the form without searching. If that is hard, it is hidden. Fill it in with your real details. See whether you get a confirmation on the screen and by email. Finally check that the enquiry reached the mailbox where you expect it and not spam.

## Common questions

**A form or only a phone number?** Both. Some people prefer to call, others write, especially in the evening or when they are at work. If you have only one, you lose the others.

**Is a chat on the website worth it?** If someone can answer quickly, yes. A chat nobody answers is worse than a form, because the person waits and gets angry. Instead you can add a button for Viber or WhatsApp.
